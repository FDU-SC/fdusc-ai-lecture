# Datacenter Buildout & Capital Expenditure, 2023 – September 2026

**Method.** Tier-1 numbers below are pulled from SEC XBRL company-facts (`PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment` / `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets`) for CIKs [789019](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000789019.json), [1652044](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001652044.json), [1018724](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001018724.json), [1326801](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001326801.json), [1341439](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001341439.json). These are **cash paid for property/equipment** and **exclude finance-lease additions** — a distinction that matters (see Microsoft note). Where a company's headline "capex incl. finance leases" exists I list it separately; **it does not reconcile exactly to the SEC series** and I could not close the gap.

## 4. xAI Colossus (Memphis)

Colossus 1 announced [2024-06-05](https://www.selc.org/news/inside-memphis-fight-against-xai/); online [2024-09-02](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1830650370336473253) with **100,000 H100s**. SemiAnalysis counted [2025-09-16](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/xais-colossus-2-first-gigawatt-datacenter) ~**200k H100/H200 + ~30k GB200** at **~300 MW**. Musk disclosed a third building ("MACROHARDRR") on [2025-12-30](https://www.reuters.com/business/musks-xai-buys-third-building-expand-ai-compute-power-2025-12-30/), taking the site to **~2 GW**; a permanent **1.2 GW** gas plant (**41 turbines**) is under construction ([Bloomberg via World-Energy](https://www.world-energy.org/article/55673.html)). A **555,000-GPU** figure circulating in trade blogs is internally inconsistent — treat as unreliable.

**Air-quality record (best-evidenced thread):** **35 unpermitted turbines** were found at Colossus 1 (aerial survey 2025-04-09); the county then permitted **15** (2025-07-02, appealed 2025-07-16). EPA confirmed on **2026-01-09** that temporary turbines require permits; MDEQ approved **41** on 2026-03-10 (appealed 2026-04-09). **NAACP v. X.AI Corp.** (N.D. Miss. 3:26-cv-00074, filed 2026-04-14) alleges 27 Southaven turbines emitting **>1,700 tons NOx/yr**; DOJ moved to intervene 2026-06-15. Turbine count: 27 → 41 → **46** (May 2026) → **57** (Jun 2026) → **69 mobile**, removal deadline Jul 2027. **No fine or settlement verified.**

## 6. Bubble criticism and the 2026 state of play

- **Michael Burry**, [CNBC 2025-11-11](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2025/11/11/big-short-investor-michael-burry-accuses-ai-hyperscalers-of-artificially-boosting-earnings.html): hyperscalers extend useful lives of compute gear — "**one of the more common frauds of the modern era**." He estimated **~$176B** of understated depreciation across **2026–2028**, with Oracle profits overstated **~27%** and Meta **~21%** by 2028. Scion's registration was terminated ~[2025-11-13](https://www.livemint.com/market/stock-market-news/michael-burry-terminates-hedge-fund-scions-registration-status-heres-what-sec-filings-show/amp-11763034154934.html).
- **Goldman Sachs**, "Too Much Spend, Too Little Benefit" (Jim Covello), *Top of Mind* issue 129, **2024-06-27** ([report PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/fe7s7tq5yawuq43sppv9a/Goldman-Sachs-Top-of-Mind-Too-Much-Spend-Too-Little-Benefit-2024.pdf)).
- **Sequoia / David Cahn:** "$200B Question" (Sept 2023) followed nine months later by a larger gap estimate ([Callodine commentary PDF](http://callodine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Callodine-Capital_Quarterly-Market-Commentary_Q2-2025_vF.pdf)).
- **MIT NANDA**, *The GenAI Divide* (Aug 2025): the "**95%** of enterprise GenAI pilots fail" claim ([Fortune, 2025-08-18](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/)) has been [publicly contested on methodology](https://www.marketingaiinstitute.com/blog/mit-study-ai-pilots).
- **The bubble has not burst as of 2026-09-16.** On **2026-09-14** the Nasdaq Composite fell **147 points (–0.6%) to 26,186**, the S&P 500 **–0.5%**, the Dow **–0.3%**, Nvidia **–3.4%**, Micron **–5.3%** — after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay urging the industry to "**pace the frontier**," echoed by Musk and Altman ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/tech-stock-selloff-ai-warning/)). The index level (26,186) is near record highs. Capital Economics forecasts the S&P 500 at **8,250** by end-2026 but expects the AI bubble to burst in 2027 with a **>20%** drop by end-2027; BofA Global Research expects five stocks (Alphabet, Apple, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia) to account for **27%** of S&P 500 growth over 12 months, and tech **50%**.
- **Verified delays/pauses:** Microsoft datacenter leases slowed per analyst reports (TD Cowen, Feb 2025) — [wire summary](https://www.schwab.wallst.com/schwab/Prospect/research/stocks/news.asp?symbol=MSFT&doc=1329-4aa4bf91-f8f2-ef11-63aa-31d006beadaf-1); Abilene expansion dropped (Mar 2026); Stargate UK paused (Apr 2026). **I could not verify any 2026 cancelled project with a specific written-off dollar amount.**
