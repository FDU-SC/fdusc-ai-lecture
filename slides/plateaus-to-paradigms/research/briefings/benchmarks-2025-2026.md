# Math & Reasoning Benchmarks, Jan 2025 – Sep 2026

Retrieved 2026-09-16. "Epoch runs" = Epoch AI's own evaluations from its public CSV ([epoch.ai/data/benchmarks.csv](https://epoch.ai/data/benchmarks.csv)); dates are run dates. Saturated: MMLU (2024), MMLU-Pro (Nov 2025), AIME/final-answer contests (2026-05-12), GPQA Diamond (2025–26), ARC-AGI-1, ARC-AGI-2 (2026-09-02), FrontierMath Tier 4 v2 (2026-08-30). ARC-AGI-3 is **not** saturated under its Standard harness (62.7%) but is at 99.9% under a Provider Adapter.

**MMLU / MMLU-Pro — saturated.** Saturated before this window, per the MMLU-Pro authors: GPT-4 reached 86.4% (Mar 2023), after which frontier models "settle at 86%–87%" ([arXiv:2406.01574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01574)). First primary-source ≥90%: o1 System Card, Dec 2024 — o1 **92.30%**, o1-preview 90.80% ([arXiv:2412.16720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16720)). Labs then dropped plain MMLU: the [GPT-5 system card](https://cdn.openai.com/gpt-5-system-card.pdf) reports only "MMLU Language (0-shot)". MMLU-Pro: 72.6% (GPT-4o, Jun 2024) → Artificial Analysis, which tags the page **"Legacy"**, lists Gemini 3 Pro Preview (high) **89.76%** (rel. 2025-11-18) and Claude Opus 4.5 Reasoning 89.45% ([AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/mmlu-pro)).

**GPQA Diamond — saturated.** Human PhD-expert baseline 69.7% (OpenAI, cited by [Epoch](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/gpqa-diamond)). Lab-reported: Gemini 2.5 Pro **86.4%** ([card](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/Model-Cards/Gemini-2-5-Pro-Model-Card.pdf), 2025-06-27); Claude Opus 4.5 **87.0%** ([card](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/64823ba7485345a7/Claude-Opus-4-5-System-Card.pdf), 2025-11-24); Gemini 3 Pro **91.9%**, Gemini 3 Deep Think 93.8% pre-release ([Google](https://blog.google/products/gemini/gemini-3/), 2025-11-18); Gemini 3.1 Pro Thinking High **94.3%** ([DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/pro/), Feb 2026). Independently (Epoch runs): GPT-6 Astra (max) **95.8%**, Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) 95.4% (2026-08-30→09-02). o3 High is 83.3% in Google's card.

**AIME 2024/2025/2026 — saturated; contamination is the story.** [MathArena](https://matharena.ai/) (ETH Zurich SRI + INSAIT) runs **no-tools, single-attempt, 30 problems**, flagging rows ⚠️ when the model postdates the contest. AIME 2025, **pre-release models only**: o3-mini (high) 86.67%, o1 (medium) 81.67%, DeepSeek-R1 70.00%, GPT-4o 11.67%. AIME 2026 pre-release: GPT-5.2 (high) **98.33%**, Gemini 3 Flash / Claude Opus 4.6 96.67%, Gemini 3 Pro 91.67%; post-release GPT-5.5 (xhigh) **100%**, Claude Opus 4.8 (max) **100%** ([tables](https://matharena.ai/competitions)). The contamination signal: GPT-5.2 maxes AIME 2025 at 100% but drops to 98.33% on the newer, unseen AIME 2026. AIME 2026 **was** adopted as a benchmark. Epoch runs OTIS Mock AIME **with a Python tool**: GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1 and Qwen3.8 Max all **100%** (2026-08-30→09-02). Saturation declared 2026-05-12: of 176 fresh qualifying problems, Gemini 3.1 Pro solved **all 176 at least once in four attempts** — final-answer contests are "no longer an effective source of hard benchmark tasks" ([MathArena](https://matharena.ai/no_final_answer/)).

**HLE — Jan 2025, not yet saturated.** [arXiv:2501.14249](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249) (v1 24 Jan 2025); published Nature **649, 1139–1146**; finalized at 2,500 questions 2025-04-03; dynamic fork **HLE-Rolling** 2025-10-08 ([lastexam.ai](https://lastexam.ai/)). Release leaders: DeepSeek-R1 8.5%, o1 8.0%, GPT-4o 2.7%. The official leaderboard is stale, topping at Gemini 3 Pro 38.3%, GPT-5 25.3%, Grok 4 24.5%. Epoch's tracker: Gemini 2.5 Pro Exp 18.2% (2025-03-25) → GPT-5 Pro 31.6% (2025-10-07) → Gemini 3 Pro 37.5% (2025-11-18) → Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview 46.4% (2026-02-19) → **Claude Fable 5.1 (xhigh) 46.5% (2026-09-01)** = SOTA. The paper's guess of >50% by end-2025 did not happen. **No "HLE 2" exists**; "HLE-Verified" (Gemini 3.8 Flash 54.9%, GPT-5.6 Sol 54.5%) is a **third-party** re-verification, not CAIS/Scale.

**FrontierMath — Tier 4 near-saturated.** **v2 released 2026-06-12**: Epoch corrected errors in 42% of problems and removed 12; the set is now **338 = 295 (Tiers 1–3) + 43 (Tier 4)**. Scoring is automated — the model submits a Python `answer()` with a Python tool and a 1M-token budget, so "solved" means a correct answer, **not** a verified proof. Funded by OpenAI, which has exclusive access to a subset (disclosed conflict of interest) ([hub](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath-tier-4-v2)). Epoch runs on the original private set: Gemini 2.5 Pro 14.1% → Gemini 3 Pro Preview 37.6% (2025-11-21) → GPT-5.2 (xhigh) 40.7% (2025-12-13) → GPT-5.5 Pro 52.4% (2026-04-23). Tier 4 **v1**: GPT-5 Pro 14.6% (2025-10-09) → GPT-5.2 Pro 31.3% (2025-12-24) → **gdm-ai-co-mathematician 47.9% (2026-05-08)**, DeepMind's AI co-mathematician. Tier 4 **v2**: **GPT-6 Astra 97.6%** (2026-08-30), Claude Fable 5.1 87.8% (2026-09-01). New: **FrontierMath Erdős** (Lean; problems open as of Aug 2026) and Open Problems.

**ARC-AGI-1/2/3.** From ARC Prize's verified leaderboard JSON (semi-private). **ARC-AGI-1 saturated**: Claude Fable 5 (Max) and GPT-6 Astra 98.5%, above the 98% Human Panel. **ARC-AGI-2**: o3 (High) 6.5% (Apr 2025) → Gemini 3 Pro 31.1% (2025-11-18) → Opus 4.5 37.6% (2025-11-24) → Poetiq refinement 54% @$30.57 (2025-12-04) → GPT-5.2 Pro 54.2% (2025-12-11) → Opus 4.6 69.2% (2026-02-05) → GPT-5.5 85.0% (2026-04-22) → GPT-5.6 Sol 92.5% (2026-07-09) → **GPT-6 Astra (Max) 95.0% @$1.12/task (rel. 2026-09-02)**. ARC Prize 2025 (2025-12-05) left the Grand Prize unclaimed; top Kaggle score 24.03% (NVARC) @$0.20/task. **ARC-AGI-3 launched 2026-03-25**: humans 100%, frontier AI **0.51%**; by 2026-09-02 GPT-6 Astra reaches **62.7%** (Standard harness, $26K) and **99.9%** (Provider Adapter, $19K) ([ARC](https://arcprize.org/blog/astra)). ARC Prize is a nonprofit: semi-private set under zero-retention agreements, $10K cap, one run per evaluation, **no tools by default**, academic panel, and ±10/±3/±15pp public-vs-semi-private agreement gates ([policy](https://arcprize.org/policy)).

**IMO 2025 / 2026.** IMO 2025 (Australia): OpenAI's experimental reasoning LLM scored **35/42** (5 of 6), two 4.5-hour sessions, **no tools or internet**, natural-language proofs, graded by three former IMO medalists with unanimous consensus; announced 2025-07-19. Google's **Gemini Deep Think** also **35/42** on the same five, no tools, announced 2025-07-21, "officially graded and certified by IMO coordinators" (Hassabis). The IMO board asked labs to wait until after the award ceremony; OpenAI said it complied, noted the IMO had earlier invited it to a formal **Lean** version (declined), and that it "was never approached about a natural language math option". Only 6 of 630 humans scored 7/7 on Q6. MathArena's re-grade of *public* models gives GPT-5 (high) 38.10%, Gemini 2.5 Pro 31.55% — the gold-medal models were never released. IMO 2026 (Shanghai, 2026-07-15/16, ~685 students): Xiaohongshu's **dots-note-3.0** scored **42/42** under IMO committee grading — the first perfect model score, 13 above the gold cut-off (29); Huawei's **Xiaoyi** also announced 42/42, with problems released after each day's exam and all human intervention forbidden ([China Daily](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202607/22/WS6a607452a310d709c2fbf0ec.html), [Huawei/PCPOP](https://www.pcpop.com/article/6942575.shtml)). MathArena had no IMO 2026 entry as of 2026-09-16.

## Unverified / low-confidence
- **OpenAI self-reported numbers are unverified** — openai.com returned 403 throughout. The widely-quoted o3 AIME figures (96.7% no-tools / 99.5% with tools) are unconfirmed; the o3/o4-mini System Card contains no AIME or HLE numbers. GPT-5's 94.6% AIME 2025 (no tools) was readable only via a search snippet. Google's card gives o3 High GPQA as 83.3%.
- **FrontierMath's original Nov-2024 release SOTA (<2%)** — not verified from an Epoch page.
- **HLE with tools** (Grok 4 Heavy 44.4%) — vendor-claimed, not on the official leaderboard.
- **IMO 2026 scores** rest on Chinese state media plus a Huawei corporate announcement; no IMO-published document found. Huawei's 42/42 is marketing-adjacent.
- Gemini 3 Deep Think's 93.8% GPQA and DeepMind's competitor tables are vendor-compiled; the Gemini 2.5 Deep Think card's table is a raster image.
- **scale.com / labs.scale.com return 403**, so the SEAL HLE leaderboard was not read directly.
- ARC-AGI-2's human baseline is inconsistent: "Human Panel 100% @$17/task" vs Epoch's "average human score of 60%".
- SEO aggregators (benchlm.ai, aiwiki.ai, llm-stats.com) were **deliberately not used**.
