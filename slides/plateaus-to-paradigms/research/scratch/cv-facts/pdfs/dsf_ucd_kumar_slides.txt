                  Core Idea
Extracting and comparing “high-level” visual features or
traits of a face image

Insensitive to image conditions

Vector of outputs from the trait classifiers are
computed on each face independently
         “Attribute” classifier
Use binary classifier trained to recognize the presence
or absence of describable aspects of visual appearance

Gender, age, nose type, skin, race, hair color, eye type
            “Simile” classifier
Removes the manual labeling required for attribute
classification

Instead, simile classifier learns the similarity of faces, or
regions of faces, to specific reference people.
